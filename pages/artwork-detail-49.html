Wave in Stillness: Walking with Geumboki at Dosan Park

This painting captures the calm and clear wave (padong) that arises within me during the most tranquil moments. The path through Dosan Park, walked alongside my loving dog Geumboki, is for me a time of deep meditation beyond a simple stroll. The winding paths and trees with red vitality symbolize the unending flow of life and the warm affection we share together. On that path where all the world's noise has subsided, I receive the silent comfort of my dog Geumboki and quietly observe my own heart. This work speaks of how life is always healed through beautiful companionship, shown through the small vibrations moving within the stillness.
